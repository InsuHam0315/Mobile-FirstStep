## Behavior and technologies

The debug launcher **소개와 퀴즈 (개발용)** provides introduction, waiting, three-question quiz and result screens for **디지털정보관 · 소프트웨어학과 (검증 전 예시)**. Korean disclosures identify direct debug entry, unverified arrival, synthetic content and device-local storage. Scripts/questions remain replaceable assets; they are not verified campus facts.

Java/XML Views render controller snapshots and emit actions. A composition host runs the unchanged H02 `ProgressService` and real Room repository on a single worker executor; storage, clocks, TTS and alarms stay outside Views. H01 tokens are reused. No dependency/build-file or main-manifest change was needed; the additive debug manifest declares the launcher, notification permission, TTS service visibility and non-exported reminder receiver. Existing launchers and H02/H03 source remain unchanged.

Playback is user-triggered through **다시 듣기** for the current sentence. Manual next changes the caption; explicit finish or skip records introduction completion through H02. Entering, pausing, speech errors and recreation do not finish it. Replay preserves the original completion timestamp. Speech stops on pause/exit and generation tokens reject stale callbacks. Readable captions remain available without a Korean voice or after injected initialization/playback failures. This is not a claim of automatic narration or verified audible Korean quality.

NORMAL eligibility is stored `introDoneAt + 15 minutes`; the foreground countdown derives from an injected clock, without repeated database polling. DEMO instant eligibility is visible and isolated from NORMAL. H02 owns shuffled attempt snapshots, 2/3 passing, immediate retry and atomic single-stamp rewards. Selected stable option IDs, sentence position, replay intent and displayed result survive ordinary recreation. Pending submissions retain their attempt/version/answers for reconciliation after resume. Storage errors are visible/retryable and corrupt records are retained.

Notifications are optional inexact `AlarmManager.set(RTC_WAKEUP)` reminders, requested by an explicit relevant action. Immutable PendingIntent identity includes mode/building, and the receiver validates it. Repeated scheduling replaces the same identity; replay does not automatically schedule. Re-entry reloads stored eligibility. No exact-alarm access, boot rescheduling or precise delivery guarantee is introduced.
